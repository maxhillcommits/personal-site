Zcash Thesis

Bitcoin’s decentralization was a great breakthrough, but it came with a fatal flaw: the lack of privacy. Satoshi stated that “a much better, easier, more convenient implementation of Bitcoin” would be possible with zero knowledge proofs. Before he disappeared, he admitted to not knowing how to implement these ZK proofs into the system.

In the age of AI and big data, that flaw has become a prison.

Zcash has successfully cracked zero knowledge proofs, turning Satoshi’s idea into reality. It brings true privacy and scalability to Bitcoin’s design.

Bitcoin is insurance against fiat. Zcash is insurance against Bitcoin. The state started printing money, so we bought Bitcoin. Then the state started surveilling money, so we bought Zcash.


Edward Snowden (December 2017): “There's nothing else in the digital currency space like the cryptographic technique underlying Zcash. It's a moonshot: risky, but certain to change everything if it succeeds.”
